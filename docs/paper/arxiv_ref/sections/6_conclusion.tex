\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:concl}

We presented \sname, a LiDAR localization stack whose contribution is a specific integration of established components rather than a new algorithm: three known estimator families, likelihood-field MCL, NDT-MCL \citep{saarinen2013ndtmcl}, and an error-state Kalman filter fusing LiDAR, IMU, and RTK-GNSS, sit behind a single ROS~2 topic and TF contract, so that a 2D LiDAR, a 3D LiDAR, or a sensor-fused configuration is a deployment choice rather than a switch to a stack with a different interface. Each estimator is a pure C++ core with no dependence on the middleware runtime, the two MCL backends share one predict--weight--resample core that differs only in its measurement model, as in existing MCL libraries \citep{puga2024beluga}, and \code{laser2d} relocalizes deterministically by branch-and-bound \citep{hess2016realtime} over a map-sized window of non-occupied cells, up to a configurable size cap, and verifies the top candidates over several scans before committing, multi-hypothesis verification that can report a place as ambiguous. Seeded synthetic experiments show that the assembled pipelines behave as intended in closed loop, and they also expose two open problems that we report rather than hide: on large maps with repeated structure a single scan admits wrong poses, which multi-scan verification makes rare (1 of 240 synthetic queries) but does not rule out, and the ESKF covariance is overconfident, mainly in attitude. On two public datasets (NCLT and the MIT Stata Center dataset) we tested only starts from a wrong initial pose, with 12 runs per setting. \code{ndt3d} with the ground grid and tempered likelihood recovers from wrong initial headings where a 6-DoF scan-to-map NDT tracker does not, although the particle filter is given the initial spread and the tracker is more accurate once converged; branch-and-bound relocalization finds the pose more often than the global localization of \code{nav2\_amcl} (9 of 12 against 3 of 12 on Stata), and \code{laser2d} converges faster than AMCL there in the runs where it converges, but not more often from offset starts. AMCL with Nav2 defaults fails on the sparse NCLT projection, \code{fusion3d} has no public evaluation, and we make no field-accuracy claim. Evaluating \code{fusion3d} on public data, more AMCL parameter sets, and further external systems remains future work. \sname{} is released under the Apache-2.0 license at \url{https://github.com/kjungmo/prism_loc}.
