These result files come from runs on public datasets and on synthetic data.

- NCLT data are used under the Open Database License 1.0 (database) and the Database Contents License (contents). Source: Carlevaris-Bianco et al., IJRR 2016.
- MIT Stata Center data are used under CC BY 3.0 Unported. Source: Fallon et al., IJRR 2013.
- Result files under `experiments/results/` that contain ground-truth poses derived from NCLT are distributed under ODbL 1.0. Those derived from Stata are distributed under CC BY 3.0. They are not under the Apache-2.0 licence of the repository.
- Synthetic-data results are under the repository licence (Apache-2.0).
- The public datasets themselves are not part of the repository. `jobs.txt` files use `$NCLT_ROOT` for the dataset directory.
